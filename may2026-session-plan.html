Session Plan (50 min)

2 min

Phase 1 — Greeting + frame today

"Two big things today: (1) one full warm-up of what CAPE looks like — same format as Wednesday's test. And (2) we'll write a journal entry just like CAPE Q19, in the new app where everything stays visible."

Don't say "we made it easier" — frame as "we made it smoother so you can focus on writing, not clicking."

10 min

Phase 2 — Tab 1: CAPE Warm-Up 📖

Step 1 (1 min) — Word map. Three persistent CAPE-irregular misses: through · league · foreign. One card each, tricky-part highlighted in red. Quick tap-through.

Step 2 (1 min) — Tool rehearsal. Show the two test buttons: 🟨 Highlighter and ✗ Answer Eliminator. Tap each. Same as Apr 29: "Highlight the sentence that ANSWERS. X out 1-2 wrong choices first."

Step 3 (3 min) — Read the passage. "What Tessa Found Beyond the Tide Pool" (9 paragraphs, sea-turtle protagonist, ~235 words). Marcello reads silently, then aloud. NO TTS — no read-aloud accommodation. Note prosody informally. Tap "I read it."

Step 4 (5 min) — Two real EBSR items.

  • Q1 (RL.3.4 vocabulary): "What does weary mean in paragraph 4?" Correct: C (tired). Part B: B ("She did not paddle in slow circles anymore. She floated.")
  • Q2 (RL.3.2 central message): "What is the central message?" Correct: B (You grow stronger when you face hard things). Part B: C ("By evening, Tessa was tired. She was also bigger, somehow.")
  • Teach: both parts must be right for full credit. The rule again — Part B is the receipts for Part A.

Step 5 — Show Q19-style NWT prompt. "Write Tessa's journal entry about her first night after the reef. Include information about how Tessa responded to the events in the story." (Pause for him to spot the connection.) "Yeah — that's exactly what we're doing today."

6 min

Phase 3 — Tab 2: Retell Tessa's Story 🔍

This replaces Apr 29's "Look Back at Monday" — today is fresh transfer, not revision.

Step 1 (2 min) — Listen to story aloud once more. Tutor reads the passage aloud (this is fine — it's modeling, not student test prep).

Step 2 (4 min) — 4 sticky notes (Lindgren-style). Marcello fills each with a short phrase, not a full sentence:

  • 📍 WHERE — where does the story start?
  • 👥 WHO — who came to find her?
  • 🤔 DECIDED — what did she decide to do?
  • ⚡ HAPPENED — what happened at the reef?

Why this works: warming up the part of the brain that tracks "what happened in this story" makes the writing easier. Don't skip even if he wants to dive straight into Plan.

6 min

Phase 4 — Tab 3: Plan the Journal 📓

Q19 prompt is shown at top of Plan tab as reminder. Read it aloud once: "Write Tessa's journal entry about her first night after the reef..."

3-question planner with territory cards (4th is stretch):

  • S1 — WHERE + WHEN, no feeling. "Tonight I am ___" (sentence starter prefilled). NO big moment, NO feeling.
  • S2 — ONE thing seen/done + ONE feeling NOT happy/joyful. Pick ONE event. Pick ONE feeling from the strong/uncertain banks.
  • S3 — NEXT event + DIFFERENT feeling. A new event, a different feeling word from S2.
  • S4 — Closing thought (stretch). "What does Tessa want to remember?"

If he tries to put everything in S1, point at the territory card. "S1's job is just where + when. The big moment is S2's job. Save it."

22 min

Phase 5 — Tab 4: Write the Journal ✍️ REDESIGNED

Single-surface layout — all 3 sentences visible at once in a vertical stack. No tab-by-tab consume-once. Each sentence card has:

  • Territory header — "MUST cover" + "MUST NOT yet" pinned at top of the card.
  • Single textarea with 🎤 mic button (webkitSpeechRecognition → text drops in directly).
  • Live check indicators on the side: Topic · Felt · Purpose · NEW · 1-sentence — light up as he types.
  • Collapsible "Scaffolds" panel — feeling chips (paragraph-level grey-out: words used in S2 are dimmed in S3's chip set), SEE/HEAR/FEEL stems for S2.
  • Collapsible "Show example" + lock button per sentence.

Plan stays visible on right-side sidebar (collapsible on mobile). Full paragraph preview at bottom shows the running journal as he writes — no flipping back to see what he wrote in S1.

⚠️ Watch the feeling-chip greying. If it doesn't actually push him beyond happy/joyful, narrate it: "Look — the bright chips are the ones you haven't used yet. Pick from there."

⚠️ If S1 sneaks in a feeling word, point at the territory card. "Where + when only. Save the feeling for S2."

⚠️ If the NEW indicator turns red (4+ letter word repeats from earlier sentence), help him rewrite the repeated phrase. The NEW check is the Apr 27 anti-loop.

4 min

Phase 6 — Tab 5: Stars ⭐

Stars only — NO Wish today. Today is the celebration. (Apr 29 reasoning was Compare-view; today's reasoning is "you're ready, here's the moment.")

  • Read whole journal aloud (TTS is fine here — it's HIS paragraph being read back).
  • Tap stars on sentences he's proud of.
  • Pep banner: "Wednesday looks just like this. You're ready."

Don't add anything new here. The shape of Wednesday — passage, EBSRs, journal entry — is exactly the loop we just ran. Name it. Then close.
